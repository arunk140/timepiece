# Privacy Policy for Timepiece - Glyph Toys

**Effective Date:** October 18, 2025

Your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to user privacy for the [Your App Name] mobile application (the "App").

This policy is simple: **we do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data.**

### 1. No Data Collection

The App is designed to function entirely offline. It does not collect, transmit, or store any personal or sensitive user data. This includes, but is not limited to:
*   Your name, email address, or other contact information.
*   Your location data.
*   Your device identifiers (like IMEI, Advertising ID, etc.).
*   Your usage data, analytics, or crash reports.
*   Any data from other applications on your device.

### 2. Internet Connection

The App **does not connect to the internet**. It operates completely on your device. Because it does not establish any network connections, it is impossible for the App to transmit any data from your device to us or any third party.

### 3. Permissions

To provide its core functionality, the App may request certain permissions:

*   **Read Next Alarm:** The App requires permission to read the system's next scheduled alarm. This information is used **only** to display the alarm time on the Glyph Interface. This data is read-only, processed exclusively on your device, and is never stored, shared, or transmitted off the device.

No other sensitive permissions are required.

### 4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with or use any third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that could collect data from you.

### 5. Children's Privacy

We do not collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since the App does not collect **any** data from **any** user, it is safe for all audiences.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
